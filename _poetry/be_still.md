---
title: "Nothing to be."
date: 2024-03-12
display-date: Spring 2024
location: Somewhere On The Road, Spain
layout: poem
lang: en
---

Sit still.  
Make equal  
what you have been told is you  
and what is outside.  

As it is all  
in the same space:  
the before  
and the behind your eyes.  

The tingling,  
the pain,  
the sound,  
the rain.  

Look back  
at what is guiding you through,  
as your face  
is just not you.  

Be still.
And there will be nothing to be.
