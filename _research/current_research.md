I am a research assistant at the [GeoSense Lab](https://uni-freiburg.de/enr-geosense/) at the University of Freiburg’s Faculty of Environment and Natural Resources, led by [Teja Kattenborn](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pgzIvAcAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=ao).

### Global Plant Trait Maps

My work contributes to global plant functional-trait modelling, building on the published study [*Crowdsourced biodiversity monitoring fills gaps in global plant trait mapping*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-68996-y). The project combines crowdsourced biodiversity observations with Earth-observation predictors to map plant functional traits globally.

I am currently exploring new modelling approaches, including foundation models; publishing the resulting data according to FAIR principles through the [ESA Open Science Data Catalogue](https://opensciencedata.esa.int/products/global-plant-trait-maps/collection); and developing a new viewer to make the trait products easier to explore and use.

This collaboration is co-supervised by [Teja Kattenborn](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=pgzIvAcAAAAJ&hl=en) and [Daniel Lusk](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=Tp6k3j8AAAAJ&hl=en) at GeoSense Lab, and [Lennart Purucker](https://ml.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/profile/purucker/) at the AutoML Lab.

### 3DTrees Data Discovery

I have also begun contributing to [3Dtrees.earth](https://3dtrees.earth/), a platform for processing, visualising, and sharing 3D forest point-cloud data. My current work focuses on extracting datasets for machine-learning applications and discovering new datasets suitable for inclusion in the platform.
